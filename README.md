How to Start a Career in Data Science and AI in Hyderabad in 2026.


Introduction

Hyderabad is not only an IT outsourcing hub but also a hub for other sectors. In 2026, companies in HITEC City, Gachibowli and Kukatpally are actively recruiting data scientists, AI engineers and business analysts and many are hiring freshers who have the proper training and project experience.

If you are not sure if data science is a suitable career path for you, this guide explains it in a straightforward manner and outlines a realistic way to begin your career in Hyderabad this year.


Is a tech background required to get started?

No. The majority of the students enrolling in any data science course in hyderabad are from engineering, commerce or even non-technical backgrounds. The most important thing is your desire to learn Python, real data and logical thinking.

You have got some experience in software testing or Selenium automation, so you are in a good position. Testers with Python and machine learning skills are getting hybrid positions like AI test automation engineer, which is one of the top growing job profiles in Hyderabad.


The Honest Roadmap for 2026.

Month 1 and 2: Build your foundation.

Begin with class 12 level statistics, basic Python syntax and SQL. You do not need to be a mathematician. Just need to know averages, probability and data structure. This is included in the first few weeks at most good institutes in Hyderabad, so you can go on to the advanced topics without being overwhelmed.

Months 3 and 4: Learn the core skills.

That is where machine learning algorithms, data visualization with Matplotlib and Seaborn and exploratory data analysis come in. A good data science course will have at least 4-6 datasets to work on, not just theory slides.

Month 5 and 6: Projects and placement prep.

No certificate is needed to get a job in Hyderabad. Projects do. Create at least 2 projects, such as a sales prediction model and customer churn analysis. Upload them to GitHub. Optimize your LinkedIn profile by incorporating keywords that are relevant to your job search including terms like data analyst, Python and machine learning.

FAQs

Can a fresher pursue a career in Data Science in Hyderabad in 2026?
Yes. Freshers from an engineering background, commerce background and even from other non-technical fields can join Data Science provided that they are familiar with basics of mathematics and have interest in learning Python and Analytics.

Is a coding background required for joining a Data Science course in Hyderabad?
No. Most Data Science courses in Hyderabad start with the fundamentals and teach Python step by step. No coding background is required before starting a course.

How long does a data science course usually take?
Most Data Science courses last for about 3-6 months including online classes and placement.

Can software testers switch to Data Science and AI in Hyderabad?
Yes. Many software testers who learn Python and ML concepts in Data Science get into positions like AI Test Automation Engineer or Data-driven QA Analyst.
